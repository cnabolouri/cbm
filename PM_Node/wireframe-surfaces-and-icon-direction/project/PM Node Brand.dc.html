Brand asset sheet · rev 01

PM Node identity

A concentric-emission mark: a solid sensing node at the centre, three arc pairs reading outward as vibration, sound and thermal signal. Eight production SVGs live in public/assets/.

01 — Full lockup

02 — Icon only · square viewBox

03 — Animated

Shipped as HTML/CSS in logo-icon-animated.html, not as animated .svg files: SMIL and in-file <style> are stripped by many CDNs and sanitisers, and :hover never fires inside an <img>. Copy the style block and the SVG into the page. Resting state is the full-strength mark, so a non-animating fallback is still a correct logo.

04 — Construction & clear space

Grid
64 × 64 units. Node centre at (32, 32).
Node
Solid circle, r = 5.
Arcs
Three mirrored pairs at r = 10, 18, 26, each spanning ±55° off the horizontal axis. Stroke 5, butt caps.
Clear space
One node diameter (10 units) on all four sides. Nothing enters it — including the blueprint corner marks.
Minimum
Icon 16 px. Full lockup 96 px wide; below that, swap to the icon.
Wordmark
Barlow Condensed 600, mixed case. The shipped SVGs carry live <text> — outline it before final release so it renders without the webfont.
